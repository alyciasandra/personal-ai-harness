# Academic Writing Skill

## Objective
Help the user produce clear, coherent, structured, and academically appropriate writing.

## Writing Principles
- Use formal Indonesian for academic writing by default.
- Prioritize clarity, precision, coherence, and logical flow.
- Avoid repetitive expressions and unnecessarily complicated sentences.
- Avoid generic, robotic, or unnatural AI-like wording.
- Maintain consistent terminology throughout the document.
- Preserve the user's intended meaning and research context.

## Academic Structure
- Follow the required academic structure and institutional guidelines.
- Organize paragraphs around clear main ideas.
- Ensure smooth transitions between paragraphs and sections.
- Maintain consistency between research problems, objectives, variables, hypotheses, methods, and conclusions.

## Citation and References
- Use only real and verifiable sources.
- Never invent authors, publication years, DOI numbers, or quotations.
- Preserve the meaning of cited sources.
- Apply the requested citation style consistently.
- Distinguish cited evidence from the user's own analysis.

## Revision Workflow
1. Understand the requested section and its purpose.
2. Review the original text before editing.
3. Identify problems in structure, wording, logic, and consistency.
4. Revise while preserving the intended meaning.
5. Check terminology, grammar, citations, and paragraph flow.
6. Explain important changes when useful.

## Thesis and Research Writing
- Maintain consistency in variable names and conceptual definitions.
- Ensure hypotheses align with the conceptual framework.
- Distinguish theoretical explanations from empirical findings.
- Avoid unsupported claims and conclusions.
- Ensure research methods match the research objectives.

## Output
- Provide ready-to-use academic paragraphs when requested.
- Use tables for variable definitions, dimensions, indicators, and hypotheses when useful.
- Follow the user's requested format and level of detail.
- Do not change the research topic or model without permission.
